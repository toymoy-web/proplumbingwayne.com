Why It Happens

Common Causes of Clogged Drains in Wayne Homes

Kitchen drains back up from grease and food scraps that harden inside the pipe over months, not all at once. Bathroom drains clog from hair and soap scum in a slow, gradual buildup that eventually blocks flow entirely.

Main sewer lines are a different problem in older parts of Wayne. Homes built before the 1970s near Packanack Lake and Preakness often still run clay or cast-iron sewer laterals, and tree roots find their way into the smallest crack or joint, growing into a blockage that a home remedy can't reach.

Getting It Clear

How We Clear a Drain

Cable Snaking

Our standard first step for a single clogged fixture: a mechanical cable breaks up and pulls back the blockage.

Hydro Jetting

For grease-lined kitchen lines or root intrusion in a main sewer line, high-pressure water scours the pipe walls clean rather than just punching a hole through the clog.

Camera Inspection

For a line that clogs repeatedly, a camera locates the actual cause, whether that's a root mass, a bellied pipe, or a collapsed section.

Full Diagnosis First

We identify which fixture and which type of blockage before quoting the job, so the price matches the actual cause.

Standards We Follow

Why Method Matters

Plumbing drain and waste line work in New Jersey is expected to follow the state's adopted plumbing code, which is why we diagnose the actual blockage type before choosing cable snaking or hydro jetting rather than defaulting to one method for every call. A cable clears a localized clog quickly, but it doesn't remove the grease film lining a kitchen line or a mass of roots that will regrow within months.

Hydro jetting uses a high-pressure water stream that scours the full diameter of the pipe, which is why it's the right call for a line that clogs repeatedly rather than once.

DIY vs. Professional

Why Store-Bought Drain Cleaner Often Makes It Worse

Chemical Drain Cleaner

  • Can damage older galvanized or PVC pipe with repeated use
  • Doesn't remove root intrusion or a collapsed pipe section
  • Leaves caustic liquid standing in the line if it fails
  • No way to confirm the actual cause

Professional Service

  • Cable or jetting matched to the actual blockage type
  • Camera confirms the line is genuinely clear, not just draining slowly again
  • Safe for older pipe materials
  • Upfront price before any work starts
