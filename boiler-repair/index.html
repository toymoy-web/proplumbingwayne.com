Signs Something's Wrong

Signs Your Boiler Needs Service

  • ✓
    No heat, or uneven heat across rooms that used to warm evenly.
  • ✓
    Banging or knocking pipes, often trapped air or mineral buildup in an older system.
  • ✓
    Pressure that keeps dropping, which usually means a slow leak somewhere in the system.
  • ✓
    Water pooling near the unit, a sign of a failing seal, valve, or heat exchanger.
  • ✓
    The pilot light won't stay lit, often a thermocouple or gas valve issue on older units.
Two Systems, One Complaint

Why "No Heat" Means Different Things Here

Wayne's mix of older steam systems and newer hot-water boilers means the same complaint, no heat, can point to very different causes depending on which system a home has. A steam system that's banging and knocking usually means trapped air or a water level that's off, while a hot-water system making the same noise more often points to sediment buildup or a failing circulator pump.

New Jersey licenses plumbers and heating technicians through the state's Board of Examiners of Master Plumbers, and boiler and gas-fired appliance work is expected to follow national fuel gas code practices. We work to those standards on every job, whether it's a quick pilot-light fix or a full system diagnosis.

What We Check

How We Diagnose a Boiler Problem

Pressure Test

Confirms whether the system is holding pressure correctly or losing it through a leak.

Ignition & Controls

Checks the pilot, thermocouple, or electronic ignition and the thermostat's control signal.

Circulation

Verifies the pump and zone valves are actually moving hot water or steam through the system.

Visual Inspection

Looks for corrosion, mineral scale, and any active leak around fittings and the heat exchanger.

Repair vs. Replace

Repair or Replace an Older Boiler?

Likely a Repair

  • Single failed part: valve, thermocouple, or pump
  • System is under 15 years old
  • No history of repeated failures

Worth Discussing Replacement

  • Multiple repairs in the past two years
  • Visible corrosion on the heat exchanger
  • Rising fuel bills without a clear cause

We give you the repair cost and a replacement estimate side by side when both are genuinely on the table, so the decision is yours to make with real numbers in front of you rather than a sales pitch toward the more expensive option. We'll also say plainly when a repair genuinely isn't worth it anymore.

Safety First

What to Do Before We Arrive

  • ✓
    If you smell gas, leave the house immediately, don't operate any switches or the thermostat, and call your gas utility's emergency line or 911 from outside before calling us.
  • ✓
    If the boiler is actively leaking water, shut it off at the emergency switch (usually a red switch near the unit or at the top of the basement stairs) rather than trying to stop the leak yourself.
  • ✓
    If you see visible flame or smoke from the unit, treat it as a fire emergency and call 911 first.
  • ✓
    Don't attempt to relight a pilot light repeatedly if it won't stay lit. That's often a sign of a failing safety component, and repeated attempts can mask a real gas-control issue.
