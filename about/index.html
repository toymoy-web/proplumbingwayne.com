About Wayne Pro Plumbing

A local plumbing business based in Wayne, NJ, serving Wayne and the surrounding Passaic County communities with upfront pricing and 24/7 emergency response.

Who We Are

A Local Team, Not a Call Center

Wayne Pro Plumbing is based at 2903 2nd St in Wayne, NJ, and we work directly in the neighborhoods we serve rather than routing calls through a national dispatch center located somewhere else entirely. When you call, you're describing your problem to someone who can schedule the right technician for your specific issue, whether that's a boiler repair, a drain backup, or an emergency call in the middle of the night.

What We Do

Our Services

We handle residential and light-commercial plumbing across Wayne and the surrounding Passaic County area: boiler and water heater repair, drain and sewer work, sump pumps and basement waterproofing, and everyday plumbing repair and installation. Large new-construction plumbing contracts are outside our scope, and we'll tell you that upfront rather than take on a job we're not set up to do well.

How We Work

Our Approach

Upfront Pricing

Every job gets a flat, written price before any work begins, so you know the cost before committing.

24/7 Emergency Dispatch

Our emergency line is answered around the clock, including nights, weekends, and holidays.

Licensed Technicians

Work is performed to New Jersey's state plumbing licensing standards.

Honest Assessments

We tell you honestly when a repair makes more sense than a replacement, and when it doesn't.

Why Local Knowledge Matters

Built Around Wayne's Real Plumbing Challenges

Wayne's real estate and geography shape the plumbing issues we see every day: Passaic River flood risk in low-lying areas, older housing stock near the lakes with galvanized pipe and stone foundations, and a heating mix of steam and hot-water boilers that requires different expertise than a standard forced-air system. We built our service lineup around these specific, real conditions rather than a generic national template that doesn't account for what actually shows up in a Wayne home.

Our Standards

What We Won't Compromise On

  • ✓
    A price before any work starts. We diagnose the problem and give you a flat, written number before touching anything, so there are no surprises on the invoice.
  • ✓
    Real limitations, stated honestly. If a job is outside our scope, like a large new-construction plumbing contract, we say so upfront rather than taking work we're not equipped to handle well.
  • ✓
    Repair-vs-replace decisions that favor you. When a repair genuinely makes more sense than a replacement, that's what we recommend, even though a replacement would cost more.
  • ✓
    Permit compliance. When a job requires a Wayne Township permit, we handle that coordination rather than skipping it to save time.
Who We Serve

Homeowners, Landlords, and Small Businesses

Most of our calls come from homeowners dealing with an everyday repair or an emergency, but we also work regularly with property managers and landlords coordinating maintenance across rental units, and with small businesses that need a plumber who can work around their operating hours without disrupting customers. Whichever category you fall into, the same upfront-pricing policy and the same licensed technicians apply.

Continuing to Learn

Staying Current on What Affects Wayne Homeowners

Plumbing codes, state regulations, and even the housing stock in Wayne itself keep changing, and we try to stay ahead of what actually affects our customers, from New Jersey's 2031 lead and galvanized service line replacement law to Wayne Township's own flood preparedness guidance for homes near the Passaic River. We write about topics like these on our blog, not as generic filler content, but because they're the specific issues our customers actually ask about.
