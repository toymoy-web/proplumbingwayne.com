Local Background

What Is Documented About Preakness

The name Preakness is tied to Preakness Valley, where Passaic County runs Preakness Valley Park. Of the park's 377 acres, 298 are in Wayne and 79 are in neighboring Totowa. Its public golf course opened in 1931 and is described as the first public course in Passaic County.

The Dey Mansion stands on the park grounds. It was built around 1770, served twice as a headquarters for George Washington during the Revolutionary War, and has been county owned since 1930.

Preakness Hills Country Club on Ratzer Road is a separate private club nearby. None of these landmarks tells us the age, layout, or plumbing of the surrounding homes.

Sources: public encyclopedia entries for Preakness Valley and Dey Mansion, and Passaic County park information, read November 5, 2026.
