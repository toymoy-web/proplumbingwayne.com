Local Landmarks

A Quieter Borough on Wayne's Northern Edge

Incorporated in 1901, North Haledon has stayed a lower-density, more residential borough than some of its immediate neighbors, with larger lots and fewer of the tightly packed row-style homes common closer to Paterson. Its housing stock spans several eras of construction, from mid-century single-family homes to newer builds, which means the plumbing calls we get here vary more in scope than in some of the more uniformly built boroughs nearby.
