Next Door Along Route 46

A Township Split Between Business and Home

Fairfield was known as Caldwell Township until a 1963 referendum gave it its current name. It has the lowest population density of any municipality in Essex County, with Interstate 80 and Route 46 cutting through it.

The Passaic River forms part of the township's border, so lower-lying properties deal with some of the same groundwater issues we see in Wayne.

What Makes Fairfield Different

Airport, Highways, and Industrial Streets

Essex County Airport sits in Fairfield, and the township's Route 46 corridor is lined with hotels, office buildings, and industrial properties that Wayne and Fairfield share. Residential neighborhoods fill in between the commercial strips. With roughly 7,900 residents, the township is smaller than its business footprint suggests.
