Local Background

What Is Documented About Pines Lake

Pines Lake is an unincorporated community and census-designated place within Wayne Township, Passaic County. The 2020 census counted 3,033 residents and 1,131 housing units there. It is served by ZIP code 07470.

It began in the 1920s as a vacation community around a man-made lake roughly 1.5 miles long. Many early homes were modified log cabins, and some built in the 1930s are year-round homes today. Community life centers on the Pines Lake Association, which runs swimming and sailing programs.

A census boundary is not always the same as the area residents call Pines Lake, so we treat the street address as the deciding fact, not the map line.

Sources: 2020 US Census figures and the public encyclopedia entry for Pines Lake, New Jersey, read November 5, 2026.

General Plumbing Information

Plumbing Questions That Come Up Around Lakes

  • ✓
    Homes first built for seasonal use. A house that began as a cabin and was later expanded can have plumbing added in more than one stage. We do not assume what is inside a wall. We look at what is actually installed.
  • ✓
    Lots near water or a slope. Groundwater and runoff can reach a basement more easily on some lots than others. Whether yours does depends on grading, foundation, and the sump pump. Testing a pump before heavy rain is cheap insurance.
  • ✓
    Shoreline and shared areas. The association manages shared lake amenities. It does not manage the plumbing inside your home, but check with it first if work could touch the shoreline or common property.
  • ✓
    Lightly used or closed-up rooms. Any room or building that sits unheated in winter should have its water shut off and drained. A frozen line is far easier to prevent than to repair.
