Mountain on One Side, River on the Other

A Borough Formerly Known as West Paterson

Woodland Park was incorporated in 1914 as West Paterson and took its current name in 2009. Garret Mountain forms its eastern edge and the Passaic River its western edge, with about a third of the borough kept as open space.

The borough has roughly 13,500 residents, and its mix of hillside streets and low riverside blocks means both drainage and heating calls are common.

Before We Arrive

According to standard plumbing emergency response guidelines, relieving pressure after closing the main can slow a leak while you wait. On hillside streets, remember that water from an upper floor can reach a finished basement faster than you expect.

On the Map

Garret Mountain and the Highway Network

Garret Mountain Reservation covers 568 acres across Woodland Park and Paterson, and Lambert Castle, built in 1893 by silk manufacturer Catholina Lambert, sits on the mountain. Interstate 80, Route 46, and Route 3 all reach the borough, which is why it is quick to get to from Wayne even though Totowa lies in between.

One ZIP, Two Towns

Shared With Little Falls

Woodland Park does not have a ZIP code of its own. It shares 07424 with Little Falls, and the borough has been working with the Postal Service for a separate one. Until that changes, mail and many online listings blur the two towns together, so it helps to tell us your street and not only your ZIP code when you call.
