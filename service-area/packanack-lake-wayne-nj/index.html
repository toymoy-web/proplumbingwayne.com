Local Background

What Is Documented About Packanack Lake

Packanack Lake is an unincorporated community and census-designated place within Wayne Township, Passaic County. The 2020 census counted 6,261 residents and 2,143 housing units, and 39.3 percent of households included children under 18. It uses ZIP code 07470.

The land was farmland in 1925, when developer Joseph T. Castles bought 26 farms covering about 700 acres. The lake was formed in 1928 when a dam was built at its southern end. Reference sources list several sub-areas, including Packanack Estates, Packanack Manor, Packanack Ridge, and Packanack Woods, though these are not official boundaries.

Members of the Packanack Lake Community Association use the lake beaches and the club facilities on Osborne Terrace. As with any census area, residents may draw the neighborhood line differently, so your street address decides coverage.

Sources: 2020 US Census figures and the public encyclopedia entry for Packanack Lake, New Jersey, read November 5, 2026.
