Across the County Line

Larger Homes, Bigger Systems

Franklin Lakes was formed in 1922 from part of Franklin Township and is named for William Franklin, the last colonial governor of New Jersey. The borough covers nearly ten square miles and includes lake-named communities such as Crystal Lakes, Blauvelt Lakes, and Shadow Lake.

Becton Dickinson is headquartered here, and most homes are owner-occupied, so the plumbing we see is mostly household systems that have to run reliably for large families.

Around the Borough

Lakes, Preserve, and High Mountain Road

Franklin Lakes has its own nature preserve, a long stretch of High Mountain Road, and Route 208 running through the borough. Ramapo High School serves the community, and several named lakes and ponds sit within the borough boundaries. Most properties sit on generous lots, which means longer pipe runs and more distance between the house and the street connection.

Why It Is Different

What Larger Homes Mean for Plumbing

A house with five bathrooms and a multi-zone boiler works differently from a small ranch. Water heater capacity, recirculation, and zone valves all matter, and a single failed part can leave a whole wing without heat or hot water. We size repairs and replacements around how the household actually uses water, not a generic default.
