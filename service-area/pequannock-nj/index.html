Where the River Valley Shapes the Plumbing

The Pompton River forms the eastern edge of Pequannock Township, and the far bank is Wayne. Pompton Plains grew up in that river valley, which is why basement moisture and sump pump questions come up here as often as they do on Wayne's own low-lying streets.

The township also touches Riverdale, Kinnelon, and Lincoln Park, so a call from one corner of Pequannock can involve a street that feels like a different town entirely.

Landmarks and Local Texture

An Older Township With Real History

Pequannock Township was formed in 1740 and incorporated in 1798, which makes it one of the older municipalities in this part of North Jersey. Its former railroad station is now the Pequannock Township Museum, listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2008, and the Van Ness House in Pompton Plains is another historic site. Housing around these landmarks spans many decades, and that range of ages shapes the repairs we see.

Two ZIP Codes

Pequannock and Pompton Plains Are Both Covered

The township uses two ZIP codes. The 07444 code is Pompton Plains, the largest community with roughly 11,000 residents, and 07440 is Pequannock proper, which also reaches into part of neighboring Lincoln Park. If you are unsure which side of the line you are on, the address on your mail settles it, and either way the call reaches the same dispatch line.
