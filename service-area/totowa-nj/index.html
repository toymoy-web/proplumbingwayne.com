Local Knowledge

A Borough Carved From Wayne Township

Totowa was formed from portions of Wayne Township and the former Manchester Township in 1898, and the two towns still share a border today. Homes in the older sections near Garret Mountain Reservation date back decades, with the same era-specific plumbing, galvanized supply lines, aging boilers, cast-iron drain lines, that we work on throughout Wayne's own older neighborhoods.
