What This Covers

The Repairs That Don't Fit a Specific Category

Some plumbing problems are specific enough to have their own dedicated service: a boiler, a water heater, a clogged drain. Others are smaller, more varied issues that don't need a specialist name: a shutoff valve that won't turn, a minor pipe fitting leak, a strange noise in the walls when water runs, or a small repair left over from a previous job elsewhere. General plumbing repair is where those calls go, and we treat them with the same diagnostic approach as any named service.

Calling us for a problem you can't quite name is never a wrong call. Part of the value of a general repair visit is that we sort out what's actually going on, rather than you needing to self-diagnose and pick the "correct" service from a list before you can even book an appointment.

Water Hammer

That Banging Sound When a Faucet Shuts Off

Water hammer, the loud bang that happens when a fast-closing valve suddenly stops moving water in the pipe, is more than just an annoying noise. Over time, the pressure spike it creates can loosen fittings and stress pipe joints throughout the system. A missing or failed air chamber near a washing machine or dishwasher connection is the most common cause, and it's usually a straightforward fix once identified.

You Don't Need to Diagnose It Yourself First

A lot of homeowners hesitate to call about a vague symptom because they're not sure it's "worth" a service call, or they worry about sounding unclear on the phone. Neither should stop you. A strange sound, an intermittent drip, or pressure that seems a little off are all legitimate reasons to call, and figuring out what's actually happening is part of the job we do, not something you need to work out on your own beforehand.
