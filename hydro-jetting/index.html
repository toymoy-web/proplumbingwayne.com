If This Sounds Familiar

Line Keeps Clogging Again After Snaking?

A lot of the calls we get for hydro jetting start the same way: a line was snaked, it drained fine for a few weeks, and then it clogged again. That pattern usually means the cable cleared a path through the blockage without removing what actually caused it, whether that's grease buildup lining a kitchen line or tree roots that have grown back into a sewer joint. Hydro jetting solves that specific problem by scouring the entire pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through.

Grease & Roots

The Two Most Common Reasons a Line Needs Jetting

Kitchen lines build up grease slowly, one meal at a time, until the pipe's usable diameter has shrunk enough that even normal use backs up. A cable pushes through that buildup without removing it, which is why the same kitchen line often clogs again within weeks of a standard snaking.

Sewer laterals face a different problem: tree roots find their way into the smallest crack or joint in an older clay or cast-iron pipe and grow into a real obstruction over time. Jetting cuts through root intrusion far more thoroughly than a cable, though a line with serious root damage sometimes needs a camera-confirmed repair alongside the jetting rather than jetting alone.
