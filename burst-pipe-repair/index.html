Why It Happens

Why Pipes Burst in Wayne Homes

Freezing is the most common cause during Wayne's winters. Water expands as it turns to ice inside a pipe, and that pressure splits the pipe wall, usually somewhere the pipe runs through an exterior wall, an unheated crawlspace, or near an uninsulated attic.

Older galvanized supply lines are the other common cause, where decades of internal corrosion thin the pipe wall until ordinary water pressure is enough to split it, with no freeze involved at all. Both causes point to a different fix, which is why we trace the actual failure before quoting the repair rather than assuming it's a simple patch job.

Freeze Risk in Wayne

Which Pipes Are Most at Risk

The pipes most likely to freeze and split are the ones farthest from a home's heated interior: supply lines running through an unheated crawlspace, a pipe against an uninsulated exterior wall, or an outdoor spigot line that wasn't shut off and drained before winter. Older homes near Wayne's lakes with less modern insulation are at somewhat higher risk than newer construction, simply because the pipe runs are less protected from outside temperature swings.

The International Plumbing Code, which New Jersey's state plumbing standards are based on, calls for water piping in unheated spaces to be protected from freezing, whether through insulation, heat tape, or routing decisions made at construction. A pipe that keeps freezing in the same spot every winter usually means that protection was never installed correctly in the first place, and we'll flag that during the repair rather than just fixing the same break again next year.

The Repair

How We Fix a Burst Pipe

Locate & Isolate

Confirm the exact break point and isolate just that section if possible, rather than leaving the whole house without water.

Cut & Replace

Remove the damaged section and replace it with pipe matched to the existing material where practical.

Pressure Test

Confirm the repair holds under normal water pressure before we consider the job done.

Prevention Check

Flag other exposed pipe runs at similar freeze risk so a repeat failure doesn't catch you off guard next winter.
