Before You Call

What Helps Us Help You Faster

Having a few details ready speeds things up: what's happening (no heat, a leak, a clog), roughly where in the house, and whether it's an active emergency or something we can schedule. For an active emergency like a burst pipe or flooding, shutting off the main water valve before you call, if it's safe to do so, is the single most useful first step.
